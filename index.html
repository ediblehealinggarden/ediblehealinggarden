    About us

    Food equity, nutrition education, and access to nature

    The Edible Healing Garden is a hospital-based community garden to help promote food equity, provide nutrition and gardening education, and improve cultural diversity and inclusivity in the greater Los Angeles area to the medically underserved.

    This garden intends to be a healing environment that also addresses nutrition insecurity in the population at the Los Angeles General Hospital (LAGH) by empowering families with nutrition knowledge, food sovereignty, and access to nature.

    LA General Medical Center, 2020 Zonal Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90033

    Getting here

    Park for free in the medical center lot on Zonal Ave. From the lot, follow the path on the map down the side of the building to the garden.

    When

    Weekdays, by appointment. Plan a visit with us, or follow @EdibleHealingGarden for events and volunteer days.

    Who

    Everyone ages 3 and up (with a guardian): patients and visitors, the medical community, students and interns, and gardening enthusiasts.

    Volunteer

    We welcome gardeners, cooks, students, and anyone who wants to spend a few hours outdoors helping families grow and enjoy fresh food.

    Donate

    Your support will

    • Enable nutrition, gardening, and cooking education to promote healthy lifestyles
    • Increase food equity
    • Provide a safe space for people to grow their own food and establish food sovereignty
    • Empower every family and child that struggles with food insecurity
